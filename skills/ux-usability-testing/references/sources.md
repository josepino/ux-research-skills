# Sources

Usability testing, benchmarks (success rate, time on task), 5 users, SUS and severity. Ideas rewritten in our own words.

- NN/g, How Many Test Users in a Usability Study? — https://www.nngroup.com/articles/how-many-test-users/ (5 users qualitative; 2-8 depending on cost; about 3-5 per distinct group).
- NN/g, Quantitative sample sizes — https://www.nngroup.com/articles/summary-quant-sample-sizes/ (40 participants; 28 and 15 at 90%).
- NN/g, 7 Steps to Benchmark Your Product's UX — https://www.nngroup.com/articles/product-ux-benchmarks/ (5-10 tasks; success, time, SEQ; same method across measurements).
- NN/g, Thinking Aloud — https://www.nngroup.com/articles/thinking-aloud-the-1-usability-tool/ (instruction, neutral prompts, biases).
- NN/g, How to Rate the Severity of Usability Problems — https://www.nngroup.com/articles/how-to-rate-the-severity-of-usability-problems/ (0-4 scale; frequency, impact, persistence).
- MeasuringU, SEQ — https://measuringu.com/seq10/ (item text, 7 points, average about 5.5 across more than 400 tasks).
- MeasuringU, UMUX-Lite — https://measuringu.com/umux-lite/ (two items; conversion to the SUS scale by regression; the 7-point equation is on that page, we do not replicate it here).
- Maze, Usability testing methods — https://maze.co/guides/usability-testing/methods/ ; Contentsquare, Usability testing — https://contentsquare.com/guides/usability-testing/ ; Insight7, remote usability testing — https://insight7.io/5-key-usability-testing-methods-in-ux/ (formats and variants).
- Prototypr, Lean user testing — https://blog.prototypr.io/lean-user-testing-a-complete-guide-to-testing-without-labs-experts-and-expense-5d34bb7f8723 (guerrilla and remote).
- Great Question, 26 Claude skills (the "task scenario linter" idea, skill 22) and the UXR Strategist catalog (usability test guide); inspiration only, nothing copied.

Unverified: NASA-TLX (no formulas or ranges are included); the 5-point UMUX-Lite equation; official Spanish translations of SEQ and SUS; the time threshold for offering help (a team decision, a heuristic of ours). The click-verb, hedge-word and step patterns in `scripts/lint_tasks.py` are heuristics of ours, not a validated instrument.
