## Little use or discarded
- AnswerLab "silence-is-golden": the URL returns the general blog listing, not the article.
- CNBC (first iPhone touchscreen), Skift (event debriefs), QuestionPro NPS, Qualtrics/Zonka (closed survey questions), insight7, remoterocketship, User Interviews reports on AI/strategy/reporting/affinity: tangential or belonging to another skill (surveys, synthesis, reports).

## Where sources disagree
- Gramcko reads posture and gaze as clues to emotional state; reading body language is interpretive and cultural. The skill treats it as a hypothesis to confirm by asking, not as data.
- Hall (UX Planet) includes examples with "normally" and closed questions that contradict the rules in `SKILL.md` step 3; the breakdown by axes was taken, not the wording.
- Memory: GitLab sets a practical limit of 2 months for asking about past behavior; Walter warns that anything older than a few days may already be distorted; Hall says only "too distant". The skill recommends anchoring on the most recent episode.
- Number of interviews: 5 (NN/g via Eleken, meant for usability) vs 5-10 (Looppanel, User Interviews). See session-conduct section 6.
- "When a pattern counts": 3 independent mentions (Collective Brain) vs 1/8 noise and 6/8 signal (rampstackco) vs saturation (Guest, Namey and Chen; Hennink and Kaiser, cited in Great Question) vs 5 users (NN/g, for usability). None was chosen; see `funnel-and-quality.md`.
- The "8 to 12 main questions in 45-60 min" figure is our heuristic; the third-party kit indicates a cap of 10 to 12.
