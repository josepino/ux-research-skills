# Interview guide template

Write the filled-in guide in the user's language (translate headings and labels).

```markdown
# Interview guide — [Project]
Duration: 45 min · Format: remote/in person · Interviewer: … · Note-taker: …

## Objective and research questions
- RQ1: …
- RQ2: …

## Participant profile
[Inclusion criteria]

## 0. Before starting (checklist)
- [ ] Consent sent/signed
- [ ] Recording permission confirmed
- [ ] Tool tested (audio, screen, captions if needed)
- [ ] Incentive defined
- [ ] Observers briefed (and quiet) · note-taker assigned
- [ ] Guide piloted with 1 person

## 1. Introduction (5 min)
"Hi, I'm … (and this is … , who will take notes). Thank you for your time. Today we'll talk about … It takes about … minutes.
There are no right or wrong answers; we are interested in your real experience.
With your permission we'll record, only so we don't lose details; nobody outside the team will see it.
You can stop or skip a question whenever you want. Any questions before we start? I confirm the recording has started and I have your permission."
Quick check: repeat 2-3 screener questions.

## 2. Warm-up (5 min)
- What do you do / what is a typical day like?
- How often do you do [activity]?
- Which apps/services do you use most for [activity]?

## 3. Context and motivations (10 min)  → RQ1
(mark each question: [M] must · [N] nice · [P] probe)
- Tell me about the last time you [activity]. What happened, step by step?
  - Probe: What did you do next? Why?
- What was the easiest / hardest part of that?

## 4. How they do it today (15 min)  → RQ2
- How do you do this task now? How long does it take?
- Do you have any problem with this task? Give me a recent example.
  - Probe: How does that affect your day to day? What did you do to solve it?

## 5. Reaction / opportunity (10 min, if applicable)
- What do you think of this? What do you like and what not?
- Do you see any problem? Can you picture yourself using it? Why / why not?
- What surprised you? How would you improve it?

## 6. Closing (5 min)
- Is there anything I didn't ask that you think is important?
- Thanks, what we will do with the information, next steps / incentive.

## Interviewer notes
- One question at a time; wait through the silence.
- Do not suggest answers. Do not correct the participant.
- Write down verbatim quotes and non-verbal signals.
```
