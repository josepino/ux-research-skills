# Sample interview questions (English). Mix of good and problematic on purpose.
# Lines starting with # are ignored by the linter.
1. Tell me about the last time you booked a trip online. What happened, step by step?
2. Do you like our booking app?
3. What's so beautiful about this new interface?
4. How do you usually plan a trip?
5. Would you use a feature that compares prices automatically?
6. What do you think of the usability and the design of the checkout?
7. Would you pay $9.99 a month for this?
8. How did the onboarding feel to you?
9. Why do you like the filters?
10. Walk me through the last time you compared two options before buying. What did you do first?
11. Don't you think the search is confusing?
12. What was the hardest part of that, and how did you get around it?
