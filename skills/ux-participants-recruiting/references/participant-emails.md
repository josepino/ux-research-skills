# Communications with participants

Templates for the emails in a session cycle. A Spanish copy for Spanish-speaking researchers is in `participant-emails.es.md`; when producing emails, write them in the participant's language. Fields in `{braces}` are variables: fill them in before sending. Tone: warm, clear, no jargon. Each message comes from a real person on the team, never from a "no-reply" address. **Nothing is sent until a team member reviews and approves it.**

Common rules
- One action per message, on the first screen. A subject line that says what it is and when.
- State the reason without revealing the hypothesis (see `screener-template.md`).
- Personal data: use only what is necessary; with several recipients, send individually or use blind copy.
- If there is a consent form, link it in the confirmation; do not ask for a signature on the day of the session.

## 1. Invitation / first contact
Subject: `You are invited to a conversation about {topic} ({duration} min)`
> Hi {name},
> I am {researcher} from the {product/company} team. We are learning how people {general activity} and would like to talk with you.
> What it is: a {format: interview/test} of {duration} minutes, {in person at… / by video call}, between {date window}.
> What you receive: {incentive and when it is delivered}.
> If you are interested, fill in this short form ({n} questions, {minutes} min) to see if you are a fit: {screener_link}. There are no right answers.
> Taking part is voluntary and you can withdraw whenever you like. If you prefer I contact you another way or you need any accessibility adjustment, let me know.
> Thanks, {signature}

## 2. Confirmation
Subject: `Confirmed: {date} {time} ({time zone})`
> Hi {name}, we are set for {day, date} at {time} ({time zone}), approximate duration {duration}.
> Where: {address and directions / video call link}.
> What you will do: {general description, without details that would bias}. You do not need to prepare anything.
> What to bring / have at hand: {device, connection, headphones}.
> Consent: read it whenever you like at {link}; we will go over it together at the start. Recording is optional.
> If you need to change anything: {contact and emergency phone on the day of the session}.
> {.ics file attached}

## 3. Reminders
**24 h before** (subject: `Tomorrow at {time}: your session with {product}`): date, time, time zone, link or address, who greets you and a direct contact; remind them how to reschedule.
**1 h before** (very short, by email or SMS if they agreed): `Hi {name}, we expect you at {time}. Link: {link}. If you have any trouble connecting write to me at {contact}.`

## 4. Remote access instructions (with an accessible alternative)
> For the session you will use {tool}. Join with this link a few minutes ({minutes}) early: {link}. No account is needed {confirm this is true}.
> Test your audio and camera here: {test link}. If you cannot or prefer not to use a camera, that is fine.
> We will share {screen/prototype}; we will guide you at the time.
> **If any of this is not accessible to you** (screen reader, captions, keyboard, another language, another medium), tell us and we will adapt: we can offer {a phone call, another platform, a shorter session or one with breaks, materials in advance in the format you prefer}. Nobody needs to justify an adjustment.
> Plan B if the connection fails: {call-in number / retry in 10 min}.

Before sending, test the tool with assistive technology (`accessibility-in-research.md`).

## 5. Rescheduling (by the person or by us)
Subject: `Let's reschedule your session of {date}`
> Hi {name}, {brief reason if ours: "something came up on our side"; if theirs: "no problem at all"}. I propose {option 1}, {option 2} or {option 3}; if none works, tell me which day suits you.
> If you prefer not to continue, that is also fine and we appreciate it.
If we cancel at short notice, pay the incentive anyway or offer compensation (`incentives.md`).

## 6. Thank-you + incentive
Subject: `Thank you for your time, {name}`
> Thank you for taking part. Your input helps us {concrete improvement, without promising changes}.
> Incentive: {amount/type}, sent by {channel} before {date}. If it does not arrive, write to me.
> You can ask us to delete your data or withdraw your consent at any time: {contact}.
> Would you like us to consider you for future studies? Reply "yes" and you go on the list; without a reply we will not contact you again.
Meet the payment date: paying late is what damages trust most.

## 7. Kind disqualification (screener or full quota)
Subject: `Thank you for your interest in the {product} study`
> Hi {name}, thank you for completing the form. This time we are looking for a different profile from yours, so we will not be able to include you. It has nothing to do with your answers or with you.
> If you like, we can keep you for future studies that fit your profile better: reply "yes".
Do not explain which exact criterion failed if that reveals the hypothesis or lets people "learn to pass" the screener. Do not mention the incentive as a consolation.

## 8. Follow-up (no response / after the study)
**No response**: a single reminder after 2-3 business days, shorter than the invitation; then close the thread politely and do not insist.
**Afterwards**: when you have something to share, a message with one sentence on what we learned and what changed or not. Closing the loop makes people want to come back. Before sending, check that it contains no quotes or data that identify anyone.

## Variable fields: checklist
`{name}`, `{researcher}`, `{product}`, `{topic}`, `{duration}`, `{date/time/time zone}`, `{place or link}`, `{incentive}`, `{contact}`, `{screener_link}`, `{consent_link}`, `{accessible alternative}`. Re-read that no brace is left unfilled and that dates and time zones are consistent.
