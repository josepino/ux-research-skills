# Quarterly audit of the research process

A periodic review of **how** research is done in the team (not of the findings). Do it once a quarter (a rhythm suggested by this skill; change it if your team is small) in a 60-minute meeting or a shared document.

## What to review
| Area | Questions | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Requests | Did every study have an associated decision? How long did triage take? | Intake log |
| Quality | Were plan, guide and consent reviewed? Were findings corrected after review? | `ux-insight-qa`, reviews |
| Participants | Right profiles? No-shows, overload, incentives paid on time? | Panel report (`ux-participants-recruiting`) |
| Data | Were consent, access, retention and deletion honored? Files with a standard name? | Repository, `repository-and-naming.md` |
| Repository | Is it consulted? Entries without tags, unarchived studies, old information? | Searches, gaps |
| Democratization | Who did what level? Any studies without review? Escalations? | `democratization.md` |
| Use and impact | Which decisions did we inform? Was action taken? | `ux-research-impact` |
| Retros | Were actions completed? What keeps repeating? | `study-retro.md` |
| Communication | Who reads the results? Is there a periodic summary? | Readers, replies |

## How to prioritize what you find
A short list of problems; order them by **impact** (how much it affects quality or participants), **effort** and **urgency**. Pick the 2-3 changes for the next quarter, with an owner and a date, and review them at the start of the next audit.

## Template
```markdown
# Audit {quarter}
## What was planned vs. what happened
## Findings by area (table above)
## Top 3 changes · owner · date
## Pending risks (data, ethics, adoption)
```
Sharing it outside the team: confirm before including identifiable data. Translate the headings to the user's language.

## Short newsletter (optional)
A periodic summary of what was studied, which decisions it informed and what is coming helps the work get read and reused. Less is more; link to the repository.
