# Study retro

A retro is a short conversation about **how we worked** on the study (not about what we found; that is synthesis and the report). Its goal is to make the next study better.

## When to hold it
- At the close of each study, once the report is delivered and memory is still fresh; in long studies, also halfway.
- Duration: 15 to 20 minutes (a short format of ours; adjust it if the team is large).
- Who: those who did the study and, if useful, a product or design person who observed. One person facilitates and another takes notes.
- If the study was very short or identical to a previous one, 5 minutes at the close is enough.

## Format
Five columns; each person writes alone (one idea per note) for 4-5 minutes, then the notes are read and grouped:
1. **What worked** (what we would repeat).
2. **What did not work** (friction, delays, bias).
3. **What we learned** (about method, recruiting, tools, stakeholders; not the study findings).
4. **What we will do differently** (concrete actions).
5. **What we want** (wishes: tools, support, time, access to users).
This layout is a variant of the well-known "4L" retros; the last two columns are our adaptation.

## Rules
- We talk about processes and decisions, not people.
- Every "what we will do differently" leaves with an **owner and a date** or it is not an action; at most 3 actions.
- Look at the previous retro: were the actions done?
- If sensitive matters come up (conflict, ethics, an incident with a participant), take them aside; see `ux-ethics-bias-review`.

## What to look at (triggers)
Recruiting (did the right profiles come?), plan and scope (`ux-research-plan`), session quality, notes and debrief, timelines, communication with stakeholders, use of the results (`ux-research-impact`).

## Template
```markdown
# Retro · {study} · {date} · {retro participants}
| Worked | Did not work | Learned | Will do differently | Want |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| | | | | |
## Actions (max. 3)
| Action | Owner | Date | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
## Review of the previous retro
- Action → done? → effect
```
Save it in the study folder (`debriefs/` or `retro.md`) and link it from the repository. Translate the headings to the user's language.
