## Notes
- The scorecard thresholds (about 35% moderator speech, 60 words for a long turn, cuts every ~10 minutes) are our heuristic.
- The lists of English and Spanish fillers, hedges and interruption markers in the scripts are our own working lists, not from a published standard.
- This is not legal advice.
