# Traffic-light rubric

When to read: when scoring any element. It is our own guide to organize the conversation, not an industry standard. Green = can be presented; yellow = presentable with a quick fix or a caveat; red = do not present it like this.

## Insight (mode 1)
| Criterion | Green | Yellow | Red |
|---|---|---|---|
| Type | Explains a why and a consequence | A good finding, the why is missing | A loose observation or a data point |
| Evidence | n of N stated, 2 or more sources or sessions | n of N stated but a single source | No n, or depends on 1 person without saying so |
| Dissent | Says who does not fit and why | Does not mention it although it exists | Hidden or not looked for |
| Actionable | There is a decision and a possible owner | There is a decision but no owner | Changes nothing whether true or false |
| Interpretation | Distinguished from evidence | Mixed but identifiable | Presented as fact |

## Theme (mode 2)
- Green: a claim about an experience or behavior ("They abandon sign-up before seeing value").
- Yellow: a label with nuance ("Friction in sign-up"): you see where it is going, the claim is missing.
- Red: a pure topic ("Onboarding") or a guide question turned into a title.

## Evidence (mode 3)
- Quotes: green = all verified or approximate due to light cleanup; yellow = approximate with possible change of meaning, or not found with a known cause (missing file); red = not found without explanation in a central message of the report.
- Sample: green = "k of N", no decimals, with limits; yellow = percentages with small n but with "k of N" next to them; red = percentages with decimals or projections with small n, no N stated.
- Contradictions: green = mentioned and explained; yellow = mentioned without explanation; red = none, with no evidence they were sought.

## Report (mode 4)
- Answer first: green = the first paragraph answers the question; yellow = it appears in the 2nd section; red = only at the end or missing.
- Recommendations: green = each points to a finding, with priority; yellow = some without reference; red = opinion without backing.
- Limits: green = sample, method and what is not concluded; yellow = only the sample; red = none.
- Separation of observation / interpretation / recommendation: green = distinguished; yellow = partially; red = mixed.

## Overall traffic light
Red if any red falls on a central message; yellow if there are yellows and no reds; green if everything central is green. Always return the criterion that decided it.
