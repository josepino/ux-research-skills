# Templates

When to read: when delivering the review or reordering an insight. Write headings and labels in the user's language.

## Review output
```
# Quality review: <study/report name>
Mode(s): <1 insight / 2 themes / 3 evidence / 4 report / 5 sparring>   Material reviewed: <...>   N: <...>
Overall traffic light: <green|yellow|red>  Criterion: <one line>

## What is good
- ...

## Review by element
| # | Element | Type (data/finding/insight) | Light | What I saw | Why it matters | Suggested question or fix |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## Quotes (if the script ran)

Summary: X verified, Y approximate, Z not found. Not found: <short quote -> closest match -> possible cause>

## Numeric check (sample_check.py)
Alerts found and how each was resolved or acknowledged. Spanish heading: «Chequeo de muestra».

## 3 priorities before presenting
1. ...
Decisions that remain yours: <interpretations I did not touch>
```

## Ordered insight (observation + why + consequence)
```
[Data]                 What was observed, with n of N and source: ...
[Analyst interpretation]  Why we think it happens (hypothesis | backed by ...): ...
[Consequence]          What changes if true (decision, risk or opportunity): ...
Dissent                Who does not fit and what they say: ...
Confidence             high | medium | low, because ...
```
Rule: what is under [Data] can be checked against the transcripts; what is under [Analyst interpretation] belongs to the researcher, who decides whether to keep it.

## Question that turns a topic into a claim
Template: "What do people do or say about <topic> that surprised us or contradicts what we assumed, and what does it suggest?" Variant: "What happens with <topic> at the moment of <situation> that explains <observed behavior>?"
