## Notes on the newer quiz items (30-39)
They apply the guidance of the sibling skills (`ux-usability-testing`, `ux-transcript-prep`, `ux-insight-qa`, `ux-research-impact`, `ux-research-onboarding`); the numbers they use (about 5 per qualitative round, ~20 for quantitative) come from the NN/g references in `key-concepts.md` section 6. "Report counts, not percentages, with small samples" and the confidence labels are our own heuristics, not external standards. See each sibling skill's `references/sources.md` for its citations.
