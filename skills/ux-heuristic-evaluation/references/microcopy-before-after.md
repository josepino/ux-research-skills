# Error microcopy: before and after (English and Spanish)

Complements `error-messages-and-language.md` (rubric and principles). The examples are ours and illustrative; adapt them to your tone and brand. If the product addresses users formally, keep the product's register (in Spanish: tú, usted or voseo as the product does).

Pattern: **what happened + why (if it helps) + what to do**, without blaming and without clearing what the person already typed.

## English
| Situation | Before | After | What changed |
|---|---|---|---|
| Malformed email | "Error: invalid email" | "Check the email address: something is missing after the @, for example name@example.com" | Says what is missing and gives an example |
| Short password | "Incorrect password" | "Your password needs at least 8 characters. You need 2 more." | Concrete threshold (adjust to your real rule) |
| Required field | "Field required" | "Enter your postal code so we can calculate shipping" | Explains the why |
| Payment declined | "Transaction failed. Code 402" | "Your bank declined the payment. Try another card or contact your bank. Your order details are still saved." | Probable cause, way out, reassurance |
| Email already registered | "User already exists" | "That email already has an account. [Sign in] or [Reset password]" | Offers both paths |
| Server failure | "Oops! Something went wrong" | "We couldn't save your changes. They're still on screen; try again in a few minutes." | Says what failed and what is kept |
| File too large | "Invalid file" | "The file is larger than allowed. Upload one up to X MB (PDF or JPG)." | Limit and format (fill X with your real limit) |
| Session expired | "Access denied" | "Your session expired for security. Sign in again; we saved what you typed." | No accusing tone |
| No search results | "0 results" | "We couldn't find 'red sneakers 50'. Try fewer words or browse the categories." | Suggests the next step |

## Español
| Situación | Antes | Después | Qué cambió |
|---|---|---|---|
| Email mal escrito | «Error: email inválido» | «Revisá el correo: falta algo después de la @, por ejemplo nombre@example.com» | Dice qué falta y da un ejemplo |
| Contraseña corta | «Contraseña incorrecta» | «Tu contraseña necesita al menos 8 caracteres. Te faltan 2.» | Umbral concreto (ajustalo a tu regla real) |
| Campo obligatorio | «Campo requerido» | «Escribí tu código postal para calcular el envío» | Explica el para qué |
| Pago rechazado | «Transacción fallida. Código 402» | «Tu banco rechazó el pago. Probá con otra tarjeta o contactá al banco. Tus datos del pedido siguen guardados.» | Causa probable, salida y tranquiliza |
| Email ya registrado | «El usuario ya existe» | «Ese correo ya tiene una cuenta. [Iniciar sesión] o [Recuperar contraseña]» | Ofrece los dos caminos |
| Falla del servidor | «¡Ups! Algo salió mal» | «No pudimos guardar tus cambios. Siguen acá en la pantalla; probá de nuevo en unos minutos.» | Dice qué falló y qué se conserva |
| Archivo muy pesado | «Archivo inválido» | «El archivo pesa más de lo permitido. Subí uno de hasta X MB (formato PDF o JPG).» | Límite y formato (completá X con tu límite real) |
| Sesión vencida | «Acceso denegado» | «Tu sesión venció por seguridad. Iniciá sesión de nuevo; guardamos lo que escribiste.» | Sin tono acusador |
| Sin resultados de búsqueda | «0 resultados» | «No encontramos «zapatillas rojas 50». Probá con menos palabras o mirá las categorías.» | Sugiere el siguiente paso |

Quick check before delivering a rewrite: is it understandable without technical context? Is it next to the field? Is there a way out? Does it avoid "invalid", "illegal", "incorrect" and exclamation marks? Does it keep what was typed? Note: an error that is easy to make is usually also a heuristic 5 failure; if the text is good but the error was avoidable, report a double finding.
