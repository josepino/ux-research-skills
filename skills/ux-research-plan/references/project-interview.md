# Project interview (kickoff with the researcher or requester)

**When to read it**: at the start, when the context is thin. Use it as a conversation, not a form: ask one to three questions at a time, skip what is already known (check `research-profile.md` first), and write what you learn into the plan. If an answer is missing, offer a default and mark it as "Assumption".

## The 10 questions
1. **Decision to inform.** What will you do differently depending on what we find? Who makes that decision, and by when?
2. **What is already known.** What data, past studies, support tickets, sales notes or analytics exist? What do you expect to find, and why?
3. **Users.** Who are the users of interest (new, returning, a segment)? Where can we reach them, and how many are realistically available?
4. **Product stage.** Is the product new or existing? Are we in discovery, validation or post-launch?
5. **Constraints.** Legal or privacy limits, tools you must use, approvals needed (for example an ethics committee), access restrictions.
6. **Stakeholders.** Who needs to be convinced or informed (executives, product, design, engineering)? What kind of evidence does each one trust? Are they aligned on the goal?
7. **Timeline.** When must the decision be made? How many weeks can fieldwork and analysis take? Are there fixed dates (launch, planning cycle)?
8. **Budget and help.** Money for incentives, tools and recruiting; who can help with sessions, note-taking or analysis.
9. **Risks.** What could make the study fail: no access to users, misaligned stakeholders, no one able to act on the results, a decision already made?
10. **Use of results.** Who will act on the findings, how, and how will we know it worked (metric or outcome)?

## Quick defaults if the person does not know
- Decision unknown: pause and use `sharpening-questions.md` or `intake-triage.md`.
- No budget stated: propose a minimum viable plan (see `roi-and-business-case.md`).
- No timeline: propose weeks relative to a start date and include analysis time.
- Users unknown: mark "Assumption: target profile = ..." and confirm before recruiting.

## After the interview
Summarize in 5 lines (decision, what is known, users, constraints, timeline) and ask the person to confirm before writing the plan.
