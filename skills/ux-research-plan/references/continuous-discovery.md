# Continuous discovery

**When to read it**: the team runs spaced-out studies and wants regular customer contact. Summary of secondary sources and the Product Talk page; the approach belongs to **Teresa Torres** (book *Continuous Discovery Habits*). We have not read the book: consult it for details.

## Core idea (Torres)
Product teams that have contact with customers **at least once a week** make fewer decisions without customer information. Each week they combine interviews (to find opportunities) and quick assumption tests (to evaluate solutions), instead of big isolated studies. Source: producttalk.org/continuous-discovery.

## Weekly cadence (our adaptation)
| Day | Activity |
|---|---|
| Monday | Review the tree and decide what to learn this week |
| Tuesday-Thursday | 1-2 short interviews based on concrete stories (when was the last time you...) and/or 1 assumption test |
| Friday | Synthesize in 30 min with the team (new opportunities, tested assumptions) and update the tree |

Automatic recruiting (in-app invitation or a list of willing customers) keeps the cadence from breaking; see `ux-participants-recruiting` and `ux-research-ops`.

## Opportunity-solution tree
Structure proposed by Torres: at the top the **desired outcome** (a business/product metric), below **opportunities** (customer needs, pains and desires, from general to specific), then possible **solutions** and below them **tests** of each solution's assumptions. It prevents jumping from a problem to a single solution and explains why you test what you test.

## How it fits with the plan
- The tree replaces the list of "questions" with open opportunities; the assumption map (`assumption-map.md`) feeds the tests.
- Interviews must be about past behavior, not "what would you like?" (`ux-interview-guide`).
- A big one-off study is still valid (benchmark, card sorting): continuous discovery does not replace them.

## Limits
It requires a team that can act weekly on what it learns; without that, notes pile up without decisions. With little access to customers, start every two weeks.
