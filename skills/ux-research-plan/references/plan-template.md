# Research plan template

When producing the deliverable, translate headings and labels to the user's language.

```markdown

**Owner:** ... · **Date:** ... · **Status:** draft / approved

## 1. Context and problem
[Which product, what is happening, why it matters now. Business data if it exists.]

## 2. Decision to inform
[Concrete decision + who makes it + deadline.]
- What we will do if the result is A: ...
- What we will do if it is B: ...
- What is already known (sources and date): ...

## 3. Objectives
1. ...
2. ...

## 4. Research questions
| # | Question | Objective | Method |
|---|----------|-----------|--------|
| Q1 | | O1 | |

## 4b. Assumption map
| Assumption | Category (desirability / usability / feasibility / business) | Known / believed (source) | Risk | Hypothesis and measurable signal | Method |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

- Problem: ...
- Need: ...
- Assumptions: ...
- Hypothesis: "We believe that [action/change] will achieve [outcome] for [users]. We will know when [measurable signal]."

## 6. Method and analysis
[Method, mode (moderated/unmoderated, remote/in person), tasks or instrument, how it will be analyzed.]

## 10. Risks, ethics and biases

## 11. Use of results
[Who acts, when, how impact is measured.]
```

## Checklist before accepting the plan
- [ ] Does each objective change a possible decision?
- [ ] Can each question be answered with the chosen method?
- [ ] Is the sample size coherent with the method?
- [ ] Are consent and data handling defined?
- [ ] Is there a decision with an owner and date, and do we know what will be done with each result?
- [ ] Are the assumptions with the highest risk and least evidence covered?
- [ ] Does the timeline include analysis time (usually underestimated)?
