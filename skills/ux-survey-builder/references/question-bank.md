# Survey question bank by scenario

Adapt scales to your product. Closed questions give comparable data; open questions give the why. Translate the questions into the language of your respondents.

## Website
**Closed**
- Did you easily find the information you were looking for? (Yes / No)
- How long did it take you to complete your main task? (Under 1 min / 1-3 / 3-5 / Over 5)
- How intuitive did you find the design? (1 = very confusing ... 5 = very intuitive)

**Open**
- What was the hardest thing to do on the site?
- What would you change to make your experience better?
- Was there anything that surprised you (good or bad)?

## Mobile app
**Closed**
- Do you use the app daily? (Yes / No)
- How fast does the app load? (1 = very slow ... 5 = very fast)
- Which feature do you use most? (Chat / Notifications / Settings / Other)

**Open**
- Which aspects of the app do you find frustrating?
- How would you like navigation to improve?
- What additional features do you think should be included?

## Sign-up process
**Closed**
- Did you complete sign-up on your first attempt? (Yes / No)
- How long did it take you to sign up? (Under 2 min / 2-5 / Over 5)
- Was the sign-up form clear? (1 = not at all clear ... 5 = very clear)

**Open**
- Which steps seemed unnecessary?
- Did you have any technical problem? Which one?
- How would you describe the process in one word?

## Navigation
**Closed**
- Were you able to find all the sections you were looking for? (Yes / No)
- How easy is it for you to use the main menu? (1 = very hard ... 5 = very easy)
- Do you usually use the search box? (Yes / No)

**Open**
- Which section was hardest to find?
- If you used search, did you find what you were looking for? Why or why not?
- What would you improve about the design to make navigation easier?

## New feature
**Closed**
- Did you use this feature in the last two weeks? (Yes / No)
- How useful did you find it? (1 = not at all useful ... 5 = very useful)
- Did it meet your expectations? (Yes / No / Partly)

**Open**
- What was the best and the worst thing about this feature?
- What would you have liked it to include?
- Would you recommend it to others? Why?

## Desirability study (adjectives)
Show the design or prototype and ask people to pick 3-5 adjectives from a balanced list (positive and negative), for example: modern, trustworthy, simple, boring, confusing, professional, warm, cold, cluttered, innovative, outdated, fast. With enough responses, trends appear.

## Examples of questions to avoid
| Problem | Example | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Leading | "Don't you think our design is more modern than the old one?" | "How would you describe the new design compared with the old one?" |
| Leading | "What did you like most about the new update?" | "What do you think of the new update?" |
| Double-barreled | "What do you think of the usability and the design?" | Two questions |
| Loaded wording | "How difficult is this product to use?" | "How easy or difficult was it to use?" (bipolar scale) |
| False dichotomy | "Do you prefer more colors or more animations?" | Open question, or add "neither / other" |
| Biased scale | 1 "a little" ... 5 "a lot" | Balanced bipolar scale |
