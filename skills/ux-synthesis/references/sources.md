# Sources used (ux-synthesis)

All paraphrased; none copied verbatim. The part of the skill each one improved is in parentheses. Dates: as shown on each page when read.

## Core (very useful)
- NN/g, "Data vs. Findings vs. Insights" - https://www.nngroup.com/articles/data-findings-insights-differences/ - Defines data/finding/insight with an NPS example and triangulation. (terminology, insights-and-prioritization sections 1, 4)
- NN/g, "Affinity Diagramming for Sorting UX Findings and Ideas" - https://www.nngroup.com/articles/affinity-diagram/ - 3 steps, 5-10 min of generation, duplicate notes, do not discard small clusters, dot voting. (affinity-step-by-step)
- Maze, "Thematic Analysis: A Step-by-Step Guide" - https://maze.co/blog/thematic-analysis/ - Inductive vs deductive, reflexive TA, when NOT to use it (fewer than 5-6 per group). (thematic-analysis sections 1-2)
- Contentsquare, "4 Qualitative Data Analysis Challenges" - https://contentsquare.com/guides/qualitative-data-analysis/challenges/ - Codebook with examples/non-examples, reflexivity, analysis-time >= collection-time rule, one-row-per-idea table. (thematic-analysis sections 4, 6, 7)
- Uxcel, "UX Research Analysis" - https://app.uxcel.com/courses/ux-research/ux-research-analysis-850 - Define codes before fieldwork, attitudinal vs behavioral, map findings to objectives, over-reduction, suspicious unanimity. (thematic-analysis, insights section 6)
- Looppanel, "10 Steps For User Research Analysis" - https://www.looppanel.com/blog/user-research-analysis - Example of 20 interviews + 1,000 users; finding vs insight; next steps. (insights section 8)
- Looppanel, "UX Research Synthesis Methods" - https://www.looppanel.com/blog/ux-research-synthesis - Notes (observation vs interpretation), code types, prefixes, repetition/consistency/impact, report structure. (thematic-analysis sections 3, 5)
- Condens, "Affinity Mapping in UX Research" - https://condens.io/blog/online-affinity-mapping-UX-Research/ - 9 tips: repeat, examine hard notes, metadata, asynchronous sessions. (affinity sections 3-4)
- Lyssna, "User Research Insights" - https://www.lyssna.com/blog/user-research-insights/ - The "so what?" test, 6 questions to validate an insight, finding vs insight table. (insights section 2)
- Uism, "What is an Insight and How is it Different from a Finding?" - https://uism.co.jp/en/what-is-an-insight-and-how-is-it-different-from-a-finding/ - 4 quality questions, insight as tension/need. (insights sections 1-3)
- Entropik, "How to Prioritize UX Research Findings" - https://www.entropik.io/blogs/how-to-prioritize-ux-research-findings-when-everything-feels-important - Severity, frequency, impact/effort, link to business goals. (insights section 6)
- Dovetail, "How to Synthesize Research Data" - https://dovetail.com/research/how-to-synthesize-user-research-data/ - Multi-source synthesis, taxonomy, criteria for actionable insight, contradictions. (insights sections 2, 4-5)

## Supporting
- IxDF, "Affinity Diagrams" - https://www.interaction-design.org/literature/topics/affinity-diagrams - KJ, one idea per note, "Other" group, hierarchy. (affinity)
- Lyssna, "Affinity Diagram" - https://www.lyssna.com/blog/affinity-mapping/ - 2-3 refinement rounds, silent sorting, bottom-up. (affinity)
- Maze, "Affinity Diagrams" - https://maze.co/blog/affinity-diagrams/ - 6 steps, KJ origin, uses (personas, empathy, problem statements). (affinity sections 1, 3)
- UXtweak, "What is Affinity Mapping in UX?" - https://blog.uxtweak.com/affinity-mapping/ - One note = one piece of information. (affinity section 2)
- UserTesting, "How to use affinity mapping" - https://www.usertesting.com/blog/affinity-mapping - List of common mistakes and how to present to stakeholders. (affinity section 6)
- Looppanel, "Affinity Mapping: The Ultimate Guide" and "When to Use an Affinity Diagram?" - https://www.looppanel.com/blog/affinity-mapping-guide , https://www.looppanel.com/blog/when-to-use-an-affinity-diagram - Preparation, icebreaker, analytical purpose vs ideation. (affinity sections 1-2)
- Uxcel, "Conducting Debrief Sessions" - https://app.uxcel.com/courses/ux-research/debrief-sessions-090 - A debrief after each session makes the final synthesis lighter (the process is covered by `ux-research-ops`).
- NN/g, "Quantitative User-Research Methodologies" - https://www.nngroup.com/articles/quantitative-user-research-methods/ - Order of magnitude of sample (~35+) for quantitative metrics. (insights section 4)
- Civicom, "Qualitative Research: 5 Common Mistakes" - https://www.civicommrs.com/qualitative-research-characteristics-common-mistakes/ - Causes of poor data: poor planning, execution, interpretation, organization and communication. (context on errors)
- UXinsight, "UX research prioritisation" - https://uxinsight.org/ux-research-prioritisation-avoiding-low-impact-projects/ - Prioritizing projects (not findings); warning signs. (insights section 7)
- Toptal, "How to Leverage Thematic Analysis for Better UX" - https://www.toptal.com/designers/ux-research/thematic-analysis-for-ux - Six phases, questions to review themes, thematic map, Participant/Codes/Themes sheet, dashboards case. Text retrieved via browser, summary (truncated at "define themes"). (thematic-analysis section 9)
- NN/g, Whitenton (2021), triangulation in UX research (variants: methods, metrics, analysts). (triangulation-across-methods)
- Nielsen (2000), "Why You Only Need to Test with 5 Users" - https://www.nngroup.com/articles/why-you-only-need-to-test-with-5-users/ - 31% average problem detection per user; 1-(1-0.31)^n model. (pattern-threshold)
- Guest, Bunce and Johnson (2006), "How Many Interviews Are Enough?", Field Methods; Hagaman and Wutich (2017), "How Many Interviews Are Enough to Identify Metathemes in Multisited and Cross-cultural Research?", Field Methods. Saturation figures. (pattern-threshold)
- Braun and Clarke, thematic analysis guidance (no minimum count makes a theme); Nikki Anderson, Dscout People Nerds (2022), "one third" rule of thumb; Collective Brain interview kit (2026), "3 independent mentions". (pattern-threshold)
- Great Question, guidance on synthesizing in batches with AI (10-12 calls per batch). (call-batch-synthesis)

## Limitations
The Lyssna "UX research guide" page and Maze/Uxcel pages arrived truncated in the download; only the readable part was used. Several sources are tool-vendor blogs (Dovetail, Looppanel, Condens, Maze, Entropik, Contentsquare): their figures and rules are indicative. The saturation, Nielsen and one-third figures come from the cited publications as carried over from earlier versions of this skill and not re-verified for this release; re-check them against the originals before quoting them in a formal report.
