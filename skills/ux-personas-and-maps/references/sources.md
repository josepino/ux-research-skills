## 4. Discrepancies between sources
- **Photo in the persona:** NN/g and PlaybookUX recommend a realistic photo; Roman prefers none to a stock one; Rohaise proposes removing real photos in archetypes.
- **Order of filling the empathy map:** NN/g allows any order; Gray prescribes Goal, then external, then internal. Pains/Gains: below (several templates) vs inside the head (Gray).
- **Value of personas:** Roman and Hosn defend them if there is research; Rohaise questions them on inclusion; Justinmind cites unverifiable marketing figures.
- **How many personas:** 1 to 2 (PlaybookUX), 1 up to 3 (Pixel), 3 to 5 (UX studio, UXPin, Crystallize), 3 to 7 (Uxcel). Number of interviews: 5 per persona (PlaybookUX), 10 or more (UX studio), 5 to 30 per role (UXPressia). The skill exposes the range and recommends a saturation criterion.
- **Origin of the empathy map:** Dave Gray/XPLANE (CareerFoundry, IxDF, UXPin) vs Scott Matthews 2010 popularized by Gray (Maze, Miro).
- **Does the experience map include competition and the business view?** NN/g: agnostic; UX Magazine and Eleken: yes.
- **Showing hypothesis maps to customers:** Tincher says no; NN/g proposes showing the current map to generate discussion and Bodine invites customers to workshops.
- **User journey vs customer journey:** NN/g considers them interchangeable; Slickplan distinguishes them by scope and audience.
- **Frequency of persona updates:** NN/g defines no schedule; the survey shows that those who review more often rate the impact higher (perception, not causality).
