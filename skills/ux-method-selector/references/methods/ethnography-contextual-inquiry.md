# Ethnography, field studies and contextual inquiry

**When**: discovery, when design assumptions may have blind spots or use depends on the environment (work, home, tools, other people). The phrase attributed to Margaret Mead says it best: what people say, what they do and what they say they do are different things.

**Differences**: *ethnography/field* = observing in the natural environment, minimal interference. *Contextual inquiry* = more structured and collaborative: the person is observed and interviewed while doing real tasks (NN/g sees it as almost the same as field, designed for complex systems). Three modalities of ethnography (Aufait): passive observation (shadowing without interaction), active (immersion with the team) and contextual interviews.

**Steps (contextual inquiry, UserTesting)**
1. Objectives, profiles to visit (look for diversity), sites (ideally several) and a flexible guide with open questions.
2. Informed consent and data-handling rules.
3. Observe: physical environment, tools, social interactions; decide whether to interrupt with questions or leave everything for the end.
4. Ask follow-ups about reasons and decisions ("how does this task fit into your flow?", "why do you choose this feature?").
5. Record with notes + audio/photo/video, including attitudes and tone, not just clicks.
6. Analyze with a diverse team: affinity diagrams, codes, patterns; prioritize insights; iterate and validate with participants.
Principles: focus, context, partnership (collaboration), interpretation.

**Sample**: small (the sources give no number; do not invent one); depth over quantity.

**Mistakes and mitigations**: access/logistics (offer flexibility or remote observation); observer bias (several observers, objective data before interpretations); people change their behavior when observed and what they state does not match what is observed (triangulate with interviews and validate findings with the participant); high cost and time.

**Deliverables**: field notes, photos/video, affinity diagram, prioritized insights, report with quotes.
