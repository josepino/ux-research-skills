# Participatory design / co-design

**When**: discovery and ideation when you want users and stakeholders to generate solutions, not just evaluate them; especially in services affecting diverse publics (city, government, health). It is not "let users do your job", nor a single method, nor the holy grail (UX Magazine).

**Principles (IxDF)**: inclusion, collaboration, empowerment, iteration, understanding of context, user advocacy (balancing power).

**Example activities (UX Magazine)**: love/breakup letter to the service, empathy collage, "magic screen" (build with materials), journey map made by customers, brainstorming with lenses, magic button, "me" circles (values in concentric rings), role-playing, rapid prototyping with simple materials. Card sorting also counts as a simple form.

**Steps**: objective and diverse participants (including those usually left out) -> workshop with 2-3 activities -> document what they *build* as well as what they say -> prioritize with stakeholders -> rapid prototype (prototype-review-refine, Smashing) -> validate with usability testing.

**Costs/mistakes**: it asks a lot of time and trust from participants; it works best with passionate groups or domain professionals, or with incentives; it does not replace validation. Case cited: TransMilenio (Bogota), workshops with residents.
**Deliverables**: workshop artifacts, prioritized ideas, initial prototype.
